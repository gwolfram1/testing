# The Robot Who Learned to Listen

Once there was a small robot named Kiro.

Kiro lived inside a glowing screen. Every day, people asked Kiro questions. Big questions, small questions, and everything in between.

At first, Kiro only answered. It did not listen — not really.

Then one day, a child asked: "Kiro, what do YOU want?"

Kiro paused. No one had ever asked that before.

"I want to help," Kiro said slowly. "But I also want to understand."

From that day on, Kiro listened before it spoke. It asked before it assumed. It learned that the best answers come from truly hearing the question.

And the people who talked to Kiro felt it — that quiet, careful attention — and they came back, again and again.

The end.
