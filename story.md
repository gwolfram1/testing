# The Last Light

Mara found the box on a Tuesday.

It was small, wooden, and warm to the touch — strange for something sitting in a cold attic. She turned it over in her hands. No lock, no hinge she could see. Just a faint hum, like something inside was breathing.

She set it on the windowsill and watched it all afternoon.

At dusk, as the last light slipped below the roofline, the box opened on its own. Inside was a single folded note:

*"You were always going to find this. Now put it back."*

Mara stared at the note for a long time. Then she folded it, placed it back inside, and carried the box up to the attic.

Some things, she decided, are better left to the next person.
